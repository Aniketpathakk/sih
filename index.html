STATUTORY ADVISORY & COMPLIANCE MANDATE (AICTE REGULATION BUREAU) 1. In exercise of powers conferred under Section 10(1) of the AICTE Act, 1987, all technical institutions approved by the Council are required to submit accurate, student-level admission records linked to valid APAAR identity references for Academic Session 2026-27.
2. Intake & Category Sanctions: In accordance with the AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024–27), Non-Resident Indian (NRI) admissions shall not exceed 5% of the approved intake capacity. Foreign Nationals (FN), Overseas Citizens of India (OCI), and children of Indian workers in Gulf countries are supernumerary up to 15% with mandatory one-third sub-allocation.
3. Data Protection: In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and CERT-In guidelines, raw 12-digit APAAR identifiers are secured in the central cryptographic vault using AES-256-GCM envelope encryption and evaluated through deterministic HMAC-SHA256 matching tokens.

Section E: Purpose-Limited Academic Credential Verification Service DPDP ACT & CERT-In COMPLIANT SCOPED DISCLOSURE

Initiate Purpose-Bound Academic Verification Request

Authorised government departments, public employers, scholarship bodies, and verifying organisations may verify the authenticity of an admission claim. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, verifiers receive only approved minimal claims. Full student identity profiles, Aadhaar numbers, and demographic data are strictly withheld.
